Outputs: public/earth/natural-earth-land-mask.png (1024x512 equirectangular), src/webgl/land-mask.json (same mask as row intervals, lazy-loaded in scene chunk), public/earth/earth-fallback.webp (orthographic8E/8S, generated by prepare-silver-fallback.py).
